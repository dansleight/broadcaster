# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 30, 2026

Broadcaster (broadcaster.apercu.us) is an invite-only private application.

## Information We Collect

We use Google OAuth to authenticate users. This allows us to:

- Verify your identity via your Google account
- Access your YouTube account information (as explicitly authorized by you)

We may store:

- Your Google user ID and email address
- Basic account metadata needed for the application to function

## How We Use Your Information

- To authenticate you and manage your access to the service
- To interact with the YouTube API on your behalf (only the permissions you grant)
- To provide the core functionality of the Broadcaster application

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your data.  
We only share your Google data with Google as part of the OAuth process. No third parties receive your data.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Access is limited to invited users only.

## Your Rights

You can revoke access to your Google account at any time via [Google Account Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
